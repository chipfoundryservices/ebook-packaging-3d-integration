# Chapter 8: Capital Allocation in Packaging—The Final Thesis

## The Inversion: What Destroys Long-Term Compounding in Packaging?

We have now walked the complete stack of semiconductor manufacturing:

**Book 1 (Chip):** Device physics, transistor scaling, crystalline silicon moats  
**Book 2 (Foundry):** Contract fabrication, TSMC's scale advantage, yield economics  
**Book 3 (Lithography):** EUV optics, ASML's optical monopoly, pattern generation  
**Book 4 (Etch):** Plasma physics, Lam Research's chamber monopoly, atomic sculpting  
**Book 5 (Packaging):** Heterogeneous integration, equipment moats, slow-motion compounding  

The question we must now answer is this: **How do we allocate capital across this ecosystem to maximize long-term compounding?**

The inversion reveals the answer: *What destroys long-term compounding in semiconductors?*

1. **Cyclicality** — Foundry capex is wildly cyclical (boom 2020, bust 2022, boom 2023, bust 2024). TSMC ROIC swings 35% → 15% → 35% → 15%. This kills compounding.

2. **Technological displacement** — Wire bonding (55% of K&S revenue) is dying. DRAM (30% of Samsung revenue) is being displaced by HBM. Companies that do not reinvent face 20-30% ROIC decline.

3. **Customer concentration** — TSMC is 80%+ of ASML's revenue. If TSMC's capex falls 50%, ASML's revenue falls 40%. Single-customer exposure kills ROIC stability.

4. **Margin compression** — Foundry gross margins have compressed from 50% (2018) to 45% (2024) due to competition. Equipment gross margins hold steady at 45-50% due to service moat. Equipment wins on moat durability.

5. **Capex timing mismatch** — Lithography capex peaked in 2023-2024. Now it normalizes. New capex cycle comes from packaging (2024-2028). Investors who stay in lithography equipment miss the packaging supercycle.

**The solution is not to pick one supplier. The solution is to identify the *new* capex cycle and position ahead of it.**

## Part I: The WFE Cycle Dynamics—From Litho to Packaging

### Historical WFE Cycle: Lithography Dominance (2010-2023)

For 13 years, the semiconductor equipment cycle was driven by **lithography capex**:

```
Year    Litho Capex    ASML Revenue    ASML ROIC    Status
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2010    $12B           $3.8B           22%          Rising
2015    $18B           $6.2B           28%          Peak
2020    $22B           $8.1B           32%          COVID trough
2021    $24B           $9.6B           35%          EUV ramp
2022    $26B           $14.8B          38%          AI boom
2023    $18B           $10.2B          30%          Normalization
2024E   $14B           $8.5B           22%          Decline
```

**Pattern:** ASML ROIC tracked lithography capex cycle with 1-2 year lag.

**Why?** Because:
- EUV lithography ($200M per system) was the bottleneck for 3nm, 5nm node production
- Every fab moving to new nodes required EUV orders
- ASML captured 85%+ of EUV market, driving exceptional ROIC

### Emerging WFE Cycle: Packaging Dominance (2024-2028)

The **new** cycle is driven by packaging capex:

```
Year    Packaging Capex    ASMPT+Amkly    Est. ROIC    Driver
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2024E   $8B                $2.0B          32%          Chiplet ramp
2025E   $11B               $2.8B          36%          3D NAND stacking
2026E   $13B               $3.5B          40%          Peak capex
2027E   $12B               $3.2B          38%          Plateau
2028E   $9B                $2.4B          30%          Normalization
2029E   $7B                $1.8B          24%          Decline
```

**Why packaging capex is different from lithography:**

1. **Higher ROIC sustainability:** Service revenue (68-70% margin) persists even when hardware capex falls. ASMPT service revenue = 40% of total, providing downside cushion.

2. **Multi-driver demand:** Lithography capex is driven by one factor (node scaling). Packaging capex is driven by three factors:
   - Chiplet adoption (Apple, AMD, Intel, Microsoft)
   - 3D NAND stacking (Samsung, SK Hynix, Kioxia, Micron)
   - HBM memory integration (NVIDIA, AMD, TSMC)

3. **Longer visibility:** Chiplet roadmaps extend 3-5 years. 3D NAND stacking requires 18-24 month yield ramps. Service contracts guarantee 5-7 years of revenue.

4. **Installed base lock-in:** Unlike lithography (where customers can substitute one EUV tool with another), packaging equipment is tied to specific customer processes. Switching costs = $100M+ per fab.
